      Notes & limits

        Why this needs a taste graph

        An LLM can name artists in a genre. It cannot tell you which ones share an audience with your headliner, which neighborhoods that audience lives in, or which brands they over-index on. Those are measurements, and they come from Qloo.

        What the agent does

        A tool-calling loop plans 8 steps over Qloo's Search, Insights (artist, demographics, heatmap, place, brand, tag), Tags, Trending and Analysis endpoints. Every call is shown in the trace with its parameters and result count.

        Responsible by design

        Only artist and city names are sent to Qloo — no personal data. Affinities describe aggregate audiences, not individuals. Responses are cached privately on the server.
